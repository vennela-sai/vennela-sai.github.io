Project

Satellite Trajectory Optimisation

Hybrid reinforcement learning on real TLE data

PPOHCPSAConvex Opt.Chaos Opt.CGRESSGP4Space-Track

Overview

Comparative study of PPO and classical/hybrid optimisers for satellite trajectory planning (fuel efficiency, target proximity, and collision avoidance) using live orbital elements from Space-Track.org. Co-authored with Sarada Jayan; accepted for AIP publication.

Key points

  • Environment from TLE → SGP4 propagation with thrust, fuel, and obstacle rewards
  • Benchmarks: PPO vs convex, chaos, and hybrid classical-RL methods
  • Accepted for publication at AIP
  • Outcome: PPO matched or outperformed classical baselines on fuel efficiency at comparable convergence cost

Setup

SatelliteEnv exposes position, velocity, target, fuel, and obstacles. Rewards combine fuel efficiency, target reachability, and collision diversion (with a large crash penalty). Optimisers select thrust vectors; episodes end on target, crash, fuel exhaustion, or step limit.

Findings

Reported comparisons show PPO consuming more fuel (exploration) with larger final target distance and higher collision rate in the tested regime, while HCPSA and CGRES achieve tighter target proximity and lower collision rates. The paper argues hybrid chaotic search remains competitive for nonlinear multi-objective orbital planning alongside RL.
