Project

Quantum Dynamics Platform

Simulation, circuit design, and molecular compilation

FlaskNumPyQiskitPySCFPennyLaneThree.js

Overview

A unified browser platform that couples Hamiltonian simulation, interactive circuit design, and molecular quantum compilation, addressing the usual fragmentation between chemistry toolchains and circuit IDEs. No local install required for the core experience; REST and SSE APIs expose the engine.

Key points

  • Trotterised evolution for static and time-dependent Hamiltonians
  • Drag-and-drop circuit builder with live 3D Bloch-sphere visualisation
  • Three-stage molecular compiler: integrals → fermionic operators → Jordan–Wigner
  • Supports Trotter dynamics and VQE; fidelity, purity, analytical MSE metrics
  • Outcome: unified simulation, circuit design, and molecular compilation in one real-time platform

Modules

Hamiltonian engine: Pauli-string Hamiltonians, first-order Trotter product formulas, piecewise-constant handling of time-dependent coefficients, expectation tracking, and evaluation via fidelity, purity, and MSE against analytical references.

Circuit builder: ordered unitary sequences with real-time state updates and Bloch vectors for single-qubit views. Molecular compiler: PySCF-style integral stage, fermionic algebra, Jordan–Wigner encoding to qubit Hamiltonians, then simulation or VQE optimisation, with intermediates inspectable for teaching and research.

Positioning

Unlike circuit-only web tools or chemistry libraries that demand heavy local setup, the platform keeps research-grade paths (custom integrals, multiple backends) inside one modular web stack aimed at both education and serious experimentation.
