Overview

Fraud analytics for insurance claims, built during a Data Science internship at Bajaj Finserv Health: learned structural embeddings of a multi-gigabyte temporal claims graph on CPU, then detected outliers for SIU triage with explainable scores.

Key points

  • ~50% reduction in fraud-related operational cost
  • CPU matrix factorisation avoids GPU-cost of naive deep graph models
  • Isolation Forest over embeddings for structural and behavioural outliers
  • Explainable suspiciousness metrics for SIU risk stratification

Method

A heterogeneous temporal claims graph is embedded with CPU-based matrix factorisation to capture provider–claim–code structure without GPU deep-GNN cost. Isolation Forest flags dense fraud subgraphs, temporal shifts in provider behaviour, cyclic ICD-code patterns, and Jaccard-similarity collusion cliques.

An inference engine computes min–max-scaled suspiciousness metrics and emits XAI outputs / telemetry that shortened Special Investigation Unit triage latency.
